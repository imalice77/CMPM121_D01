Student layers and desk items atlases
These transparent PNG atlases were generated as adaptations of the original study pixel atlas, retaining the OpenMoji-inspired style. They add six student appearances, an isolated writing arm/notebook, an empty room, and separately placeable supplies. The same CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and license above apply to these adapted assets.

Girl life scenes and perspective props
Additional imagegen adaptations preserve one brown-haired female protagonist across eight stages: Chinese primary/middle/high-school uniforms, university hoodies with progressively longer loose hair, tired PhD appearance with glasses, and white-collar career appearances with glasses and a long tied-back hairstyle. Environments progress from bedroom to dorm/research room and office. Writing hands are drawn proportionally within each scene; the game adds the equipped pen as an independent animated layer. Props have unified desk perspective and shadows. These adaptations retain the CC BY-SA 4.0 license and attribution above.

Typing and idle-face atlases
The typing atlas replaces each notebook activity with a central laptop and matching hands, preserving the protagonist and stage backgrounds. The idle-face atlas supplies eyes-closed relaxed expressions for time-based face animation. Both were created with imagegen as adaptations of the life-stage atlas and retain the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and license above.

Wide room and companion atlases
The wide-writing, wide-typing, and wide-companions atlases extend the life-stage scenes with larger desks, matching open/closed-eye frames, laptop activity, and companions integrated into the room perspective. The mentor is a male character with glasses and a beard. These imagegen adaptations retain the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and license above.
